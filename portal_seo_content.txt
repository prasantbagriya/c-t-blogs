What is EduExam Pro?
EduExam Pro is an enterprise-grade online examination management system built by ChatWizs Studio. It provides educational institutions with a complete digital ecosystem for managing the entire exam lifecycle — from question creation and student enrollment to secure exam delivery, automatic grading, result publishing, and performance analytics.
Unlike basic quiz tools, EduExam Pro is built for the real-world demands of schools and institutes: handling large numbers of simultaneous test-takers, maintaining exam integrity, generating detailed performance reports, and providing a smooth, professional exam experience for students on any device.
EduExam Pro is currently trusted by hundreds of educational institutions and serves thousands of students every month through its secure, high-performance platform.
Key Features of EduExam Pro Online Exam System
1. Computer-Based Testing (CBT) — Fully Secure
EduExam Pro's CBT module is the heart of the platform. It delivers a professional examination environment that mirrors real-world competitive exam interfaces. Key security features include:
Browser lockdown mode — prevents students from switching tabs or opening other applications during the exam
Question randomization — each student receives a unique set of questions from the question bank, making it virtually impossible to share answers
Option shuffling — answer choices are randomized for every student on every attempt
Time-limited access — exams automatically submit when the countdown timer expires
IP-based access control — restrict exam access to specific locations or networks
One-time login tokens — unique exam links ensure only registered students can access the test
2. Comprehensive Question Bank Builder
Building your question library has never been easier. EduExam Pro supports:
Multiple question types: MCQ (single answer), MCQ (multiple answer), True/False, Fill in the Blank, Short Answer, and Descriptive/Essay questions
Rich text editor — add images, equations (LaTeX support), tables, and formatted text to any question
Import questions in bulk from Microsoft Word or Excel
Tag questions by subject, topic, difficulty level, and marks
Reuse questions across multiple exams — build your question library once and use it forever
Question preview before publishing
3. Instant Auto-Grading & Detailed Results
One of EduExam Pro's most powerful features is its automatic grading engine. The moment a student submits their exam, the system:
Grades all objective questions (MCQ, True/False, Fill in the Blank) instantly and accurately
Calculates the total score and percentage in real time
Generates a detailed result report showing which questions were answered correctly and incorrectly
Provides answer explanations to students (configurable by teacher — can be shown immediately or after exam window closes)
Flags descriptive/essay answers for manual teacher review
Notifies students and parents by email when results are published
4. Real-Time Analytics Dashboard
EduExam Pro's analytics dashboard gives teachers, administrators, and institute management a bird's-eye view of exam performance across all students, classes, and subjects:
Class-level performance overview — average score, pass/fail rate, highest and lowest scorers
Question-level analysis — see which questions were answered correctly by most students vs. which were most commonly missed
Student-level performance tracking — monitor individual student progress across multiple exams over time
Subject and topic-wise performance breakdown
Comparative analysis — compare class performance across different exam attempts or time periods
Exportable reports in PDF and Excel format for management review or parent-teacher meetings
5. Complete Student & Teacher Management
EduExam Pro is not just an exam tool — it is a complete institute management platform:
Create unlimited student accounts with registration numbers, class assignments, and profile photos
Bulk student import from Excel spreadsheets
Assign teachers to specific classes, subjects, and exam creation roles
Role-based access control: Admin, Teacher, Invigilator, and Student roles with different permissions
Parent portal — allow parents to view their child's exam history and results
Automatic email and SMS notifications for exam schedules, results, and announcements
6. Exam Scheduling & Management
Plan and manage your entire exam calendar from one dashboard:
Set specific start and end dates and times for each exam
Automatically open and close exam access based on schedule
Allow multiple exam attempts (configurable) for practice or retake scenarios
Set grace periods for late submissions
Pause and resume exams in case of technical issues
Manage separate exam windows for different classes or batches simultaneously

Frequently Asked Questions — EduExam Pro
Q1. Is EduExam Pro completely free?
EduExam Pro offers a generous free plan that includes unlimited exam creation, up to 100 students, auto-grading, and basic analytics. Premium plans with advanced features (unlimited students, AI proctoring, custom branding, priority support) are available for larger institutions. Visit chatwizs.com/portal for current pricing details.
Q2. Does EduExam Pro support CBT (Computer-Based Testing)?
Yes. CBT is EduExam Pro's primary strength. The platform is built from the ground up for computer-based testing, with features like browser lockdown, question randomization, countdown timer, live invigilation, and instant results — all the hallmarks of a professional CBT exam environment.
Q3. Can students take exams on smartphones?
Yes. EduExam Pro is fully mobile-responsive. Students can log in and take exams on any Android or iOS smartphone, tablet, or computer using any modern browser. No app download is required.
Q4. How secure are exams on EduExam Pro?
EduExam Pro implements multiple layers of exam security: HTTPS encryption, question randomization, answer option shuffling, browser tab-switching detection, IP-based access control, time-limited access, and unique login tokens. These measures make EduExam Pro as secure as commercially available CBT platforms.
Q5. Can I import questions from Word or Excel?
Yes. EduExam Pro supports bulk question import from Microsoft Word and Excel files using a simple template. You can import hundreds of questions in minutes, saving enormous amounts of time when setting up large question banks.
Q6. How many students can take an exam simultaneously?
EduExam Pro's infrastructure is built to handle thousands of concurrent users. The platform is hosted on scalable cloud servers that automatically adjust capacity during peak exam periods, ensuring smooth performance even when large numbers of students log in at the same time.
Q7. Does EduExam Pro work without internet?
EduExam Pro is a web-based platform and requires an internet connection for both administrators and students. However, the platform is optimized for low-bandwidth environments, making it suitable for schools and institutes in areas with limited connectivity.
Q8. Can I customize the exam portal with my institute's branding?
Yes. Premium plan subscribers can customize the EduExam Pro portal with their institute's name, logo, colors, and domain. This creates a fully branded examination experience for your students.
