# ChatWiz Project: Feature Analysis & Platform Report

ChatWiz is an all-in-one AI-powered customer engagement and automation platform. Below is a detailed analysis of each platform integration and its management features.

---

## 1. Threads Management
The Threads module is designed for high-engagement social interaction on the Threads platform.
- **Post Publisher:** Create and publish threads directly from the dashboard.
- **DM Inbox:** Real-time management of direct messages.
- **Comment Manager:** Track and reply to comments on your posts to maintain engagement.
- **AI Auto-Reply:** Uses AI to automatically respond to common queries in threads and comments.
- **Insights Hub:** Detailed analytics on follower growth and post performance.
- **Recently Published Feed:** A visual history of all content uploaded via the platform.

## 2. Instagram Business Management
A comprehensive suite for managing Instagram Business profiles.
- **Universal Inbox:** Manage DMs with a specialized interface that supports quick replies.
- **Automation Flows:** Connect Instagram to custom chat flows for automated lead generation.
- **AI Intelligence:** Toggle AI auto-responses to handle customer queries 24/7.
- **Performance Analytics:** Track engagement metrics and account growth.
- **Spam Center:** Filter and manage unsolicited messages or comments.

## 3. WhatsApp Business API
The most powerful tool for direct customer communication.
- **Bulk Messaging:** Send mass updates or newsletters to contact lists.
- **Template Management:** Create and get approval for official WhatsApp message templates.
- **Flow Form Builder:** Create interactive forms within WhatsApp for data collection (e.g., booking an appointment).
- **Contact Management:** Advanced CRM to manage customer phone numbers and tags.
- **Official API Sync:** Real-time synchronization with Meta's official WhatsApp Business API.

## 4. Website Chat Widget
A customizable widget for any website to capture visitors.
- **Visual Customizer:** Change colors, icons, and positioning to match website branding.
- **Video Player:** Embed video messages to welcome users (premium feel).
- **Flow Integration:** Trigger automated chat flows as soon as a user clicks the widget.
- **Mobile Responsive:** Optimized for both desktop and mobile viewports.

## 5. Automation & AI (Core Engine)
The "Brain" of ChatWiz that works across all platforms.
- **Flow Builder:** A drag-and-drop interface to create complex automation logic (Nodes, Triggers, Actions).
- **Knowledge Hub:** Upload documents or URLs to train the AI on your specific business data.
- **Agent Setup:** Configure a specialized AI sales agent or customer support agent.
- **Handoff Logic:** Automatically transfer an AI chat to a human agent when needed (with a configurable delay).

## 6. Management & Analytics
- **Sales Dashboard:** Track conversions and revenue generated through chat interactions.
- **Campaign Analytics:** Measure the success of bulk messaging campaigns.
- **User Management:** Multi-user support with roles (Admin, Agent) for team collaboration.
- **Integration Hub:** One-click connection for Meta (Facebook, Instagram, Threads) and other third-party tools.
